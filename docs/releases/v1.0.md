The first release of **TuneTable**, a modified version of [BitChord](https://github.com/kushagrasinghx/BitChord) by kushagrasinghx.

### Download
- **TuneTable-v1.0.apk** installs on any Android phone (Android 8.0 or later).
- **TuneTable-v1.0_arm64-v8a.apk** is a smaller download for most modern phones.

TuneTable installs as its own app, alongside BitChord if you have it. Enable "Install unknown apps" for the app you download it with.

### Highlights
- **Your own accent colour** and **five styles** (Subtle, Deep, Glow, Duotone, Neon) for how it dresses the app.
- **Player styles**: the now playing screen follows the app's style, takes its own, or uses Simple's blurred cover.
- **Previews in Settings** for Style, Player style and App icon.
- **A new Home** with TuneTable's mark, a greeting, filters, your playlists, "Jump back in" and recently played.
- **Folding bars**, **progressive blur**, and album, playlist and artist pages in your style.
- **Spotify playlists** alongside your YouTube Music ones.
- **Redesigned** now playing screen, Settings, Audio output and Cast.
- **A new icon**: two Ts on a dark vinyl record, four alternates, and a themed icon for Android 13+.
