A small update to TuneTable 1.0. If you have 1.0 installed, TuneTable will offer this update itself, or install the APK over it — your settings and sign-ins stay.

### Fixed
- **Spotify playlists appear straight away** after signing in to Spotify, instead of only after reopening the app.

### Changed
- **Full-screen cover art is off by default.** The player opens with the cover in its card; turn the full-screen cover on in Settings if you prefer it.

### Download
- **TuneTable-v1.0.1.apk** installs on any Android phone (Android 8.0 or later).
- **TuneTable-v1.0.1_arm64-v8a.apk** is a smaller download for most modern phones.
