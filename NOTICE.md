## What was changed

TuneTable was forked from BitChord in November 2026 and modified by [darkvoidz](https://github.com/darkvoidz). The changes are to the app's design and presentation: the now playing screen, Home, Settings, menus, Audio output and Cast; accent colours and styles; player styles; progressive blur; the name, icon and package; and Spotify playlists in the library. Every change is recorded in this repository's commit history.

The source code for every release is this repository, at the release's tag.

## Made with AI

These modifications were made with the help of AI: the design changes and the code that implements them were written by Claude (an AI model by Anthropic), directed and reviewed by darkvoidz. The underlying app — playback, sources, lyrics, downloads, accounts and nearly everything else that makes it work — is BitChord's, by its original author and contributors.
